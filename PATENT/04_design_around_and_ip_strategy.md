# Design-Around Protection & IP Strategy

## How a competitor will try to design around you — and the counter

| Design-around attempt | Counter (already in this package) |
|---|---|
| Ship nut and sleeve **separately in one package** ("kit") to dodge the captive/unitary claim | Kit embodiment #26 + method claim 3 (covers the twist-then-heat use regardless of shipped state) |
| Use **cold-shrink** sleeve instead of heat shrink | Embodiment #14 + draft claims using "heat-recoverable **or** elastically-recoverable" language where counsel allows |
| Use **mastic band** instead of adhesive lining | Embodiment #9; claim "sealant lining" generically in at least one independent claim |
| Put the sleeve on a **push-in (WAGO-style) connector** instead of a twist-on | Embodiment #17 — instruct counsel to draft at least one claim to "an electrical connector" generically |
| Change retention: glue bead, clamp ring, overmold | Embodiments #5, #6, #7 |
| Stepped/top-hat sleeve to avoid your shrink-ratio dependent claims | Embodiment #10 (and keep ratio limits in *dependent* claims only) |
| Manufacture and sell only in China / export around US patent | CN utility model + CN customs IP recordation (below) |

## Filing strategy & timeline

| When | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| NOW | Confirm provisional filing date; calendar the 12-month deadline with 60-day buffer | Everything hangs on this date |
| Before factory contact | Non-provisional drafted (or at least NNN executed + disclosure limited to provisional content) | Unprotected disclosure = free gift |
| ≤ month 12 | File US non-provisional claiming priority + PCT (or direct CN filing) | Preserves US + international rights |
| With CN counsel | **CN utility model** in parallel (grants in ~7–12 months, no substantive exam, cheap) | The only fast, enforceable right where your factory lives; deters the factory itself |
| After CN grant | Record with China customs (GACC) | Enables seizure of infringing exports at Chinese ports |
| Anytime (cheap) | US + CN **design patent** on shipped-state appearance | Blocks lookalike knockoffs on Amazon; Amazon takedowns accept design patents readily |
| Now | US + CN **trademark** on the brand name | China is first-to-file; squatters watch new US brands |

## Practical enforcement reality

- The utility patent's biggest commercial value at your stage: **Amazon Brand Registry / IP takedowns** and deterring US distributors from stocking clones — not litigation.
- Mark packaging "Patent Pending" now; after grant, virtual marking (URL) per 35 USC 287.
- Keep an evidence file: dated prototype photos (the Figure 0–3 sheet), lab notebook, factory correspondence — supports priority disputes and derivation claims.

## Trade-secret layer (complements patents)

Keep OUT of the patent (and out of any single factory's hands if possible):
- The exact tack process window (temperature/time/fixture design) — process trade secret.
- Adhesive formulation selection rationale and supplier qualification data.
- The QC golden-sample criteria.
