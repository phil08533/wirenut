# ABSTRACT

A sealable electrical connector assembly includes a twist-on wire connector and a recoverable, sealant-lined sleeve captive on the connector. A first portion of the sleeve is retained on the connector shell, for example by partial factory heat-recovery onto the shell skirt or by a molded annular retention formation. A second portion extends beyond the connector's open end in a substantially unrecovered state, defining an open entry bell through which conductors are inserted and the connector installed exactly as a conventional twist-on connector, the sleeve rotating with the shell. Applied heat then recovers the second portion onto the conductors, activating the sealant lining to form a continuous moisture seal between shell and conductors while providing strain relief. Embodiments include a thermochromic full-recovery indicator, a stepped-diameter sleeve, a retrofit collar for standard connectors, and elastically recoverable sleeves.
