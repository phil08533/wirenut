What is claimed is:

**1.** A sealable electrical connector assembly comprising:
a wire connector comprising an insulating shell having an open end for receiving a plurality of electrical conductors and a cavity in which the conductors are mechanically and electrically joined; and
a recoverable sleeve having an interior sealant lining, the sleeve comprising:
a first portion retained on an exterior surface of the shell such that the sleeve is captive on the connector; and
a second portion extending beyond the open end in a substantially unrecovered state and defining an open entry bell having an inner diameter greater than an outer diameter of the shell at the open end, such that the conductors are insertable through the second portion into the cavity, and the connector is installable on the conductors, without displacing the sleeve;
wherein the second portion is recoverable onto the conductors after installation such that the sealant lining forms a continuous environmental seal between the shell and the conductors.

**2.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the wire connector is a twist-on wire connector comprising a helical spring disposed in the cavity, and wherein the first portion retains the sleeve against rotation relative to the shell such that the sleeve rotates with the connector during rotational installation.

**3.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the sleeve is heat-recoverable and the first portion is partially heat-recovered onto the exterior surface of the shell, the second portion remaining substantially at an as-supplied diameter.

**4.** The assembly of claim 3, wherein the sealant lining comprises a hot-melt adhesive having an activation temperature below a full-recovery temperature of the sleeve, and wherein the hot-melt adhesive within the first portion is not fully activated in the as-supplied state.

**5.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the shell comprises external grip formations, and the first portion is retained on a substantially cylindrical skirt band of the shell located between the grip formations and the open end, leaving the grip formations exposed.

**6.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the sleeve has a diametral shrink ratio of at least 3:1.

**7.** The assembly of claim 6, wherein the sleeve has a diametral shrink ratio of at least approximately 4:1.

**8.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein a fully recovered inner diameter of the second portion is no greater than approximately 85% of a diameter of a smallest conductor bundle the connector is rated to receive.

**9.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the second portion is dimensioned to extend, after full recovery onto the conductors, at least approximately 15 mm beyond the open end along the conductors.

**10.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the sleeve carries a thermochromic indicator that changes appearance upon reaching substantially a full-recovery temperature of the sleeve.

**11.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the sleeve comprises a stepped-diameter member having a proximal region of smaller diameter retained on the shell and a distal bell region of larger diameter defining the open entry bell.

**12.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the first portion is retained on the shell by a collar engaged with an exterior skirt of the shell, the collar carrying the sleeve.

**13.** The assembly of claim 1, wherein the shell comprises polyamide, the sleeve comprises ultraviolet-stabilized cross-linked polyolefin, and the sealant lining comprises a hot-melt adhesive bonding to polyvinyl chloride, polyethylene, and polyamide.

**14.** A twist-on wire connector comprising:
an insulating shell having an open end, an internal tapered cavity, and an exterior skirt portion adjacent the open end;
a helical spring disposed in the cavity for joining a plurality of conductors upon rotation of the connector; and
an annular retention formation on the skirt portion, the retention formation comprising a lead-in surface inclined relative to a longitudinal axis of the shell and facing away from the open end, and a retention surface facing toward the open end, the retention formation being dimensioned to retain a recoverable sealing sleeve on the skirt portion against axial displacement and against torsion during rotational installation of the connector.

**15.** The connector of claim 14, wherein the lead-in surface is inclined at approximately 30° to 60° to the longitudinal axis and the retention surface is substantially perpendicular to the longitudinal axis.

**16.** The connector of claim 14, further comprising a heat-recoverable, sealant-lined sleeve retained on the skirt portion by the retention formation, a portion of the sleeve extending beyond the open end in a substantially unrecovered state and defining an open conductor-entry bell.

**17.** A method of forming a sealed electrical connection, the method comprising:
providing, as a unitary article, a wire connector having an insulating shell with an open end and a recoverable, sealant-lined sleeve captive on the shell, a portion of the sleeve extending beyond the open end in a substantially unrecovered state and defining an open entry bell;
inserting stripped ends of a plurality of conductors through the open entry bell into the connector;
installing the connector on the conductors to form a mechanical and electrical joint while the sleeve remains captive on the shell; and
thereafter recovering the portion of the sleeve onto the conductors such that the sealant lining forms a continuous environmental seal between the shell and the conductors and the recovered sleeve provides strain relief to the joint.

**18.** The method of claim 17, wherein installing comprises rotating the connector, the sleeve rotating with the shell, and wherein recovering comprises applying heat to the sleeve from a flame, hot-air, or other heat source.

**19.** The method of claim 18, further comprising observing a change in appearance of a thermochromic indicator carried by the sleeve as confirmation that full recovery temperature has been reached.

**20.** The method of claim 17, wherein the plurality of conductors comprises conductors of at least two different wire gauges, and the recovered sleeve and activated sealant lining seal interstices between the conductors of different gauges.
