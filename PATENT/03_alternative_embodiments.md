# Alternative Embodiments (include in the non-provisional specification)

Every embodiment below should be described and drawn in the spec, even the ones you never intend to build — each one you describe is one a competitor can't cleanly claim or safely use.

## Attachment / retention variants

1. Partial factory pre-shrink tack onto smooth skirt (launch design).
2. Molded annular barb ring / groove on shell skirt (Concept B).
3. Multiple axial ribs or knurling on skirt instead of a ring.
4. Snap-on collar adapter carrying the sleeve (Concept D retrofit).
5. Adhesive bead or double-sided tape band as sole retention.
6. Sleeve mechanically crimped/clamped by a thin metal or plastic ring.
7. Sleeve insert-molded or overmolded onto the shell during shell molding.

## Sleeve variants

8. Dual-wall adhesive-lined polyolefin (primary).
9. Sleeve with internal mastic band instead of full adhesive lining.
10. Stepped-diameter ("top-hat") extruded sleeve: small diameter pre-formed at the nut end, large bell at wire end — reduces required shrink ratio.
11. Semi-transparent sleeve permitting visual inspection of the joint.
12. Thermochromic full-recovery indicator (stripe, dot, or full-body color change).
13. Pre-printed circuit-ID marking surface on the sleeve.
14. Cold-shrink elastomeric sleeve held expanded by a removable core (no heat needed) — important design-around blocker.
15. UV-cure or chemically-activated sealant liner instead of hot-melt.

## Connector variants

16. Standard spring-insert twist-on (primary).
17. Push-in / lever-style connector body with the same captive sleeve system (blocks the WAGO-style design-around — **high value**).
18. Crimp-sleeve connector with captive pre-tacked sleeve.
19. Oversized 3×#10 shell per the WN-XL dimension set.
20. Shell with integral wire-entry strain ribs cooperating with the recovered sleeve.

## Sealing-direction variants

21. Sleeve extending from mid-shell (as prototyped).
22. Full-shell sleeve covering the entire connector including tip.
23. Two-zone sleeve: high-adhesive lower zone on wires, adhesive-free upper zone on shell (cleaner tack process).

## Method variants

24. Heat by open flame, hot-air, induction-heated ferrule, or electrically self-heating conductive layer in the sleeve.
25. Factory pre-application onto pigtail assemblies (harness-level product).

## Kit / packaging embodiments

26. Connector + sleeve supplied unassembled as a kit with alignment feature (fallback if the captive claim is designed around by shipping parts separately).
