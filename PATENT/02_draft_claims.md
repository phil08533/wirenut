# Draft Claim Language (working draft for patent counsel)

**Disclaimer:** Drafting aid for your attorney, not legal advice and not filing-ready. Claim scope decisions belong to registered patent counsel.

## Independent claim 1 — apparatus (Concept A/B, the core)

> 1. A sealable electrical connector assembly comprising:
> a twist-on wire connector having a shell with an open end for receiving a plurality of electrical conductors and a conductive spring disposed within the shell;
> a heat-recoverable sleeve having an interior adhesive lining, the sleeve comprising:
> a first portion at least partially recovered onto an exterior surface of the shell adjacent the open end, retaining the sleeve on the connector; and
> a second portion extending beyond the open end in a substantially unrecovered state, defining an open bell through which the conductors are insertable into the connector without displacing the sleeve;
> wherein, upon application of heat after installation of the connector onto the conductors, the second portion recovers onto the conductors and the adhesive lining forms a continuous environmental seal from the shell to the conductors.

## Independent claim 2 — apparatus (Concept B anchor geometry)

> 2. A twist-on wire connector comprising a molded shell having an internal tapered cavity and a conductive spring, the shell further comprising an annular retention formation on an exterior skirt portion, the retention formation having an inclined lead-in surface and a transverse retention surface dimensioned to mechanically retain a heat-recoverable sealing sleeve against axial displacement during rotational installation of the connector.

## Independent claim 3 — method

> 3. A method of forming a sealed electrical connection, comprising:
> providing a connector assembly per claim 1 as a unitary article;
> inserting stripped ends of a plurality of conductors through the open bell into the connector;
> rotating the connector to mechanically and electrically join the conductors; and
> applying heat to recover the second portion onto the conductors and activate the adhesive lining, thereby environmentally sealing the connection and providing strain relief.

## Independent claim 4 — retrofit article (Concept D)

> 4. A sealing adapter for a twist-on wire connector, comprising a collar engageable with an exterior skirt of the connector and a heat-recoverable, adhesive-lined sleeve carried by the collar in a partially unrecovered state defining an open conductor-entry bell…

## Dependent claims (selected)

- 5. …wherein the sleeve has a shrink ratio of at least 3:1 [and separately: at least 4:1].
- 6. …wherein the first portion is recovered onto a cylindrical skirt band of the shell below external grip formations, leaving the grip formations exposed for rotation.
- 7. …wherein the second portion extends at least 15 mm beyond the open end after full recovery onto the conductors.
- 8. …wherein the sleeve carries a thermochromic indicator that changes appearance upon reaching full-recovery temperature.
- 9. …wherein the adhesive lining comprises a hot-melt adhesive having an activation temperature lower than a full-recovery temperature of the sleeve.
- 10. …wherein the shell comprises polyamide and the sleeve comprises cross-linked polyolefin rated for ultraviolet exposure.
- 11. …wherein the recovered inner diameter of the second portion is no greater than 85% of a minimum rated conductor-bundle diameter of the connector.
- 12. (to claim 2) …wherein the retention formation comprises a barb ring having a lead-in surface inclined at 30°–60° and a retention surface substantially perpendicular to the shell axis.
- 13. …wherein the unitary article is configured such that insertion and rotation are performable identically to an unsleeved twist-on connector.

## Drafting instructions for counsel

1. Spec must tell the **problem/solution story**: prior tubing must be threaded onto wires *before* joining; a twist-on connector rotates during install, which known sleeves would obstruct or wind up; the partially-recovered captive state + open bell solves both. This is the §103 defense.
2. Include the full dimension tables from `/CAD_REQUIREMENTS` and the shrink-ratio math from `/ENGINEERING/03` as embodiment support for claims 5, 7, 11.
3. Include ALL of Concepts A, B, D as embodiments (C omitted deliberately — crowded art).
4. Draw method figures directly from the prototype Figure 0–3 sequence.
5. Consider a **design patent** on the shipped-state appearance as cheap supplementary protection, and a CN utility model in parallel (see 04).
